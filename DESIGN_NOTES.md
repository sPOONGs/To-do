# Dalvi interaction direction

The user prioritizes an iPhone-like emotional feel: careful details in even small interactions.

- Design press feedback, transitions, cancellation, and arrival states alongside each new feature.
- Use restrained spring motion and subtle haptics; respect Reduce Motion.
- To later completion: compress the checkbox on press, then shrink the card toward the destination filter. Reverse completion toward Tasks or Links, depending on the item.
- Preserve touch target sizes even when the visible icons are small.
- Prevent competing gestures, especially calendar month swipes versus bottom-tab swipes.
- Persist data safely; animations must not cause lost items or duplicate actions.
